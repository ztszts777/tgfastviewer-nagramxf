# Features

Open **NagramXF Enhancements** in the plugin list to configure individual features.

## Media browsing {#media-browsing}

- **Fast browsing** prioritizes nearby photos and videos when you open a chat item. It loads more as you swipe and keeps albums together.
- **Thumbnail strip** includes items from neighboring messages. Tap the handle to show or hide it. Drag the handle to resize it. In portrait, the strip defaults to the bottom edge, starts collapsed, and remembers its state per chat.
- **Show in shared media** opens the grid near the current item and marks it. Back returns to the viewer before returning to the chat.

## AyuGram filters {#ayugram-filters}

Turn on **Apply AyuGram filters** to use each chat’s active rules, including exclusions, for chat previews, pinned messages, shared media, files, and fullscreen browsing. Empty date headers are removed, and the latest visible message can serve as the chat preview.

When you jump to the latest position in a chat whose newest loaded message is filtered out, the plugin uses NagramXF’s read flow to update the unread badge.

## Video and music {#video-and-music}

| Feature | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Video settings** | Use the gear to change playback speed, select an available quality, choose a playback mode, or turn on background audio. |
| **Playback modes** | Choose **Stop after this**, **Continuous play**, or **Loop**. Only one mode is active at a time. |
| **Background audio** | Choose **Listen in background** in the video settings. Use the top playback bar after leaving the viewer. The setting is saved per chat. |
| **Picture-in-picture and Android media controls** | Open picture-in-picture from the video menu. Playback, pause, and 15-second skips are available in the system controls. The video title and artwork can appear when available. |
| **Music toolbar** | Use the two rows of controls for playback and actions such as speed, sleep timer, and **Show in chat**. **Add to Profile** can be hidden in settings. |
| **Sleep timer** | Tap the clock in the video or music player. Choose a preset from **15 to 120 minutes** or enter a custom duration from **1 to 1440 minutes**. Turn on **Finish this item first** to let playback finish the current item when the timer expires. |

## Gestures and rotation {#gestures}

| Gesture | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Double-tap the center | Play or pause. |
| Double-tap the left or right edge | Seek backward or forward by the interval in NagramXF settings. |
| Hold the video or an empty area of the viewer | Temporarily speed up playback. The default is **2.5×**. |
| Pinch and drag | Zoom and pan. Use the reset button to return to the original size. |
| Swipe down or up in the center of a wide video | Enter or exit fullscreen, respectively. |
| Swipe vertically on the left or right | Adjust brightness or media volume, respectively. The values are restored when you leave the viewer. |

Brightness and volume haptics default to one tick per 1% change, 60% intensity, and a limit of 30 ticks per second. Opening a video wider than it is tall can rotate the viewer to landscape. Auto-rotation while swiping between media is off by default.
